# Support

* **Questions and ideas:** open an issue with the form that fits.
* **The screen does not start or a move is refused:** say what the log prints at start-up (the lines about `hmi.cfg`), what the screen says under the stop button and whether the stop input is wired.
* **Hardware questions:** see `docs/HARDWARE.md` first. If you have the schematic or a capture of the module's pins, that is the most useful thing you can share.

This is a one-person project and the screen is at its first steps: answers can take a few days.
